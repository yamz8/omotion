# Shot list

Status: the launch template (gate 2). Fill in every [bracket] from the
brief's facts; keep the beats, their states, and their times unless the
person approves a change.

STATES: OLD WAY -> PRODUCT -> WHAT IT DOES -> REAL SCREEN -> CALL TO ACTION

00.0-03.0s   HOOK
  enters: the first word, already on screen
  exits: the claim, read and held
  on screen: "[The claim, in five words.]"
  why: the claim, before anyone scrolls past

03.0-06.0s   PROBLEM
  enters: the headline clears
  exits: the old way in a small card, and nothing else
  on screen: "[What goes wrong without it.]" / "[The old way, in one line]"
  why: what goes wrong without the product

06.0-09.0s   REVEAL
  enters: the card
  exits: the card grown into what the product does, the first thing in the accent
  on screen: "[The product] does it." / "1 [First thing it does]" / "2 [Second thing]" /
    "3 [Third thing]" / "4 [Fourth thing]" / "5 [Fifth thing]" / "6 [Sixth thing]"
  why: the answer, as the thing itself

09.0-12.0s   PROOF
  enters: the list clears
  exits: the real screen, the camera close on what proves the claim
  on screen: "[What the real screen proves.]"
  why: it's real

12.0-15.0s   CLOSE
  enters: the screen clears
  exits: the logo, the name, and the call to action, held as the poster
  on screen: "[Product]" / "[Price or availability]" / "[The call to action]"
  why: what to do next

FORMATS
16:9: headline top left, the object under it across the width
9:16: everything inside the platform's safe area, stacked
1:1 and 4:5: tighter type, the same stack
