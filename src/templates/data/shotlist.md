# Shot list

Status: the data template (gate 2). Fill in every [bracket] and the numbers
in scene.qml from the brief's facts, each with its source; keep the beats,
their states, and their times unless the person approves a change.

STATES: QUESTION -> NUMBERS -> INSIGHT -> SOURCE

00.0-03.0s   QUESTION
  enters: the first word, already on screen
  exits: the question, read and held
  on screen: "[The question the numbers answer?]"
  why: what the numbers answer

03.0-07.0s   NUMBERS
  enters: the question moves up
  exits: every bar at its value, labelled
  on screen: the question / each label and value from the brief
  why: the evidence, all of it

07.0-11.0s   INSIGHT
  enters: every bar
  exits: the answer's bar in the accent, the rest dimmed, the insight above
  on screen: "[What the numbers show.]"
  why: what the numbers show

11.0-15.0s   SOURCE
  enters: the insight
  exits: the insight and the chart held, the source under them, as the poster
  on screen: "[Source: who measured it, and when]"
  why: why to believe it

FORMATS
16:9: the headline over the chart, rows across the width
9:16, 1:1, and 4:5: the same stack, with tighter type in 1:1
