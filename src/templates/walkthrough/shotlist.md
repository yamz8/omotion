# Shot list

Status: the walkthrough template (gate 2). Fill in every [bracket] from the
brief, and set each stop in scene.qml to where its step is on the real
screen; keep the beats, their states, and their times unless the person
approves a change.

STATES: TASK -> FIND -> CHOOSE -> RESULT -> CALL TO ACTION

00.0-02.5s   TITLE
  enters: the first word, already on screen
  exits: the task, read and held
  on screen: "[What the viewer will learn to do.]"
  why: what the viewer will be able to do

02.5-05.5s   FIND
  enters: the title clears
  exits: the real screen, the camera on the first step
  on screen: "1  [Where to start]"
  why: where to start

05.5-08.5s   CHOOSE
  enters: the first step
  exits: the camera on the second step
  on screen: "2  [What to choose]"
  why: the choice that matters

08.5-11.5s   RESULT
  enters: the second step
  exits: the camera on the result
  on screen: "3  [What you get]"
  why: what the viewer gets

11.5-15.0s   CLOSE
  enters: the screen clears
  exits: the logo, the name, and the call to action, held as the poster
  on screen: "[Product]" / "[The call to action]"
  why: what to do next

FORMATS
16:9: the caption over the screen, the screen across the width
9:16, 1:1, and 4:5: the screen in a tall box, cropped to its middle until the camera moves
